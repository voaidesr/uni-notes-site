$$
\mathcal{L}(\hat{\mathbf{y}}, \mathbf{y}) = \sum_{i=1}^n (\hat{y}_{i} - y_{i})^2

Unde 
$$
y_{i} = \mathbf{w}^{\top}\mathbf{x}_{i} + b
$$
Deci 
$$
\mathcal{L}(\hat{\mathbf{y}}, \mathbf{y}) = \sum_{i=1}^n (\mathbf{w}^{\top}\mathbf{x}_{i} + b - y_{i})^2
$$

\mathbf{W}_{n} =  \mathbf{W}_{n-1} - \lambda \nabla \mathcal{L}

Unde 

$$
\nabla \mathcal{L} = \begin{pmatrix}
\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial w_{1}}  \\
\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial w_{2}}
\end{pmatrix}
$$
Derivata intră în sumă, deci derivăm doar termenul din sumă 

$$
f (x_{1}, x_{2}) = (w_{1}x_{1i} + w_{2}x_{2i} + b - y_{i})^2
$$
Deci 
$$
\frac{\partial f}{\partial w_{1}} = 2(w_{1}x_{1i} + w_{2}x_{2i} + b - y_{i}) \cdot x_{1i}
$$
Analog
$$
\frac{\partial f}{\partial w_{2}} = 2(w_{1}x_{1i} + w_{2}x_{2i} + b - y_{i}) \cdot x_{2i}
$$
Deci 

$$
\nabla f = 2(\mathbf{w}^{\top}\mathbf{x}_{i} + b - y_{i})\mathbf{x}
